Broadhead • Orléans

PARTY MIX DIPA

$5.20

7.4% ABV / 75 IBUs / 8 SRM

Hops: Nectaron / Galaxy / Simcoe / Citra / Centennial / Amarillo / Cascade / Chinook & CTZ

Malts: 2 Row / Pale Malt / Pilsner Malt / Carafoam / Crystal Light & Crystal Medium

Party Mix DIPA is a throwback to our Ten Hop Triple IPA, brewed to celebrate Broadhead’s 10th anniversary.  Created to mark 15 years of Broadhead Brewing, this anniversary release brings together 15 ingredients in a classic DIPA style, celebrating the spirit of experimentation that has been part of our brewing journey from the beginning.
 
A blend of nine hop varieties brings plenty of citrus, grapefruit, orange zest, ripe mango and passionfruit, with hints of stone fruit and pine.  The malts adding in a touch of caramel sweetness, biscuit and toasted bread, balancing the expressive hop character without getting in the way.

Pouring a brilliant, clear russet orange with a light white head, this 7.4% DIPA delivers firm bitterness, a clean, dry finish and lingering notes of citrus and pine.

Fifteen ingredients for fifteen years. A fitting follow up to our original anniversary TIPA and another reason to celebrate.
