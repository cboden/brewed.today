About the Partnership

Canadian Gold celebrates Broadhead Brewing’s partnership with Team Homan. Two Ottawa brands share a love of community and sport, and Broadhead is the team’s Official Beer Partner through 2030. Brewed with curling fans in mind, it’s a beer for cheering on one of Canada’s most accomplished teams.

A Note From Jamie, CEO and Co-Founder of Broadhead Brewing Company.

"I grew up in a small Canadian town where sport was at the centre of bringing people together, and curling continues to play that role in communities across the country. At Broadhead, we've always believed beer is simply the vehicle. What really matters is creating connections and bringing people together around shared experiences. Team Homan embodies many of the qualities we admire: dedication, teamwork, perseverance and a commitment to inspiring the next generation. We're excited to build something that goes beyond sponsorship, giving fans new ways to celebrate and connect with one of Canada's most accomplished teams."
