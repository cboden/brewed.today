Rest In Peaches

$6.00 + tax

Rest In Peaches is a collaboration with Toronto's Blood Brothers Brewing. We had a lot of fun hosting "the brothers" Dustin and Brayden here to make something completely new to Slake.

This beer started its life as a normal hazy IPA, but we added peach terpenes to it. Terpenes are like essential oils which are what give something its aroma. It's what makes peachy smell... peachy.

This IPA is VERY PEACHY and we think you'll like it a lot.
