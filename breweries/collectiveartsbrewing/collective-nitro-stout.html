What's the craic? Our New Nitro Stout is the talk of the town. Everything you’d expect from a proper pint: velvety richness in every sip, creamy moustache included. At 4% ABV, it delivers balanced notes of roasted barley, black coffee, and a hint of bitter chocolate. Creamy from the first sip to the last, with that signature slow cascade that makes every pour feel like a ritual.

Our Nitro Stout features a widget inside the can that creates its signature cascading pour, velvety texture, and smooth, creamy head.

Here's how to get the perfect pour:

CHILL: Refrigerate for 24-48 hours before serving.

CRACK: Open the can when you're ready.

POUR: Immediately pour the entire can into a glass and watch the nitrogen cascade into a smooth, creamy finish.

DON'T SKIP THE POUR. The widget is designed to work its magic when you open the can, and pouring immediately into a glass is the best way to experience it.

Chill it. Crack it. Pour it. Enjoy it.
