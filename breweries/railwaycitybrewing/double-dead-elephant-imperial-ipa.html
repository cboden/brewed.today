Double Dead Elephant Imperial IPA

Our tribute to Jumbo, the largest elephant in the world and attraction of the P.T. Barnum Circus, killed in a railroad accident on Sept 15, 1885 in St. Thomas.

"Jumbo" was one of two Swahili words: "jambo" (which means "hello") or "jumbe" (which means "chief").  His name established the word "jumbo" meaning large in size.  When you raise your glass, you will enjoy everything that Jumbo was and became.  Life should be celebrated in every way, and if possible "Live Life Large" - enjoy it Jumbo sized.
