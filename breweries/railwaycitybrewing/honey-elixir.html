Honey Elixir

In the heyday of the travelling circus, medicine showmen rolled into town hawking magical elixirs said to cure every ailment imaginable. Inspired by that tradition, the Woodworth & Edwards Travelling Revival Tour proudly presents Honey Elixir.

Named for the streets surrounding St. Thomas' historic circus grounds, where Jumbo's legendary journey came to its unforgettable end, this honey brown ale is brewed with local honey from Clovermead Apiaries in nearby Aylmer.
