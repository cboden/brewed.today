Raised in Val Gagné, Claudia Charlebois is an old soul with deep musical roots, shaped by a family where music has always been a guiding force. With a lifelong passion for singing and performing, Claudia has been captivating audiences as a solo artist for the past three years, appearing at celebrated venues and festivals such as Octo-Beer Fest, The Oasis Bar, Street Fest, Iroquois Falls Carnival, Rock On the River, and many others. Blending influences from traditional country, folk, blues, and bluegrass, Claudia Charlebois brings a unique sound that honors classic favorites while introducing fresh original songs. The stage comes to life with the support of her talented band, featuring her father Cleo Charlebois, her mother Debra Charlebois, and Kurtis Edwards—each contributing their own style and heartfelt musicianship to every performance. Driven by a love of music and a desire to forge genuine connections with the audience, Claudia looks forward to every show, sharing the songs that have shaped her life and creating new memories with each performance.
